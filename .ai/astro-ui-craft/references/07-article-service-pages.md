# Article & Service Pages

## Article
Readable width, strong title hierarchy, date/update/reading metadata, TOC when useful, callouts, related posts, author block, low-pressure CTA.

## Service
Empathy opener → who it is/isn't for → what happens → format/duration → price or price anchor → process → FAQ → contact CTA.

For helper sites, clarity reduces anxiety. Do not hide basic process information merely to force contact.
