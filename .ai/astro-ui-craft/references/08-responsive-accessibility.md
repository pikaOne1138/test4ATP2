# Responsive & Accessibility

- Design mobile as a composition, not a shrunken desktop.
- Minimum practical tap target: 44×44px.
- Preserve readable body type and contrast.
- Provide visible focus states.
- Sticky/fixed CTA must not cover content.
- Disable cursor-only effects on touch.
- Collapse article TOC on mobile.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
