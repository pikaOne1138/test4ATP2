# CTA & Content

Use at most three semantic CTA levels.

## Knowledge
Primary: subscribe.
Secondary: free resource / Start Here.
Low pressure: read/explore.

## Helper
Primary: 預約初談 / 開始聊聊.
Secondary: 了解合作方式 / 第一次來？先看看流程.
Low pressure: article / free resource.

Avoid unnecessary urgency, countdowns and hard-sell language for trust-sensitive professions.
