# Visual Systems

## Paper & Ink
Warm paper, dark ink, serif display typography, fine rules, restrained rectangular geometry, low-saturation imagery.

## Morning Light
Cream background, clay/rose/sage accents, soft serif headings, large radii, gentle light/glow, natural imagery.

## Quiet Studio
Warm near-white, precise grid, sans-forward typography, one strong accent (deep green/navy/ochre), thin borders, almost no shadow.

Use design tokens for background, surface, text, muted, border, accent, radius, shadow, timing and easing.
